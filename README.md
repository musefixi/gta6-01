**Neon Harbor: Vice Run** — an original top-down open-world crime game that runs entirely from static files in a desktop browser. No build step, no network at runtime: plain HTML, CSS and ES modules on a 2D canvas.

You are Jax Marlowe, a wheelman new in Port Azura, a pastel coastal city of ten avenues, seven streets, a marina, a park and a beach. Work through fixer Dee Fontaine's six-mission story chain at Shine Auto Body, run taxi fares, race two rivals through eight checkpoints, rob the bank's score, collect 20 hidden packages, and keep one step ahead of a five-star police response that escalates from patrol cars to armed roadblocks of squad cars.

What's in the city:

- A full driving model — acceleration, braking, speed-sensitive steering, handbrake drifts, skid marks, smoke, fire and wrecks — plus on-foot movement, sprinting, mouse aim, fists and a purchasable pistol with tracers and reloads.
- Living traffic on a lane-correct road grid that brakes, panics and honks; parked cars to steal (two always wait by your safehouse); pedestrians who stroll, hail taxis, flee danger and drop cash.
- A wanted system with five heat bands: police pursue with lead-the-target steering, officers get out and give chase at two stars, and they open fire at three. Break line of sight to cool down, or pay for a respray.
- Shops and services: Harbor Arms (pistol and ammo), Shine Auto Body (respray and heat cut), St. Solana General (healing), the Marlowe Safehouse (heal, save, stash progress in localStorage).
- A 260-second day/night cycle with dusk tints, streetlight pools and headlights; a circular minimap with police and objective blips; money, clock, wanted stars, health/armour, weapon, speedometer and car-condition HUD.
- Fully synthesized WebAudio sound — engines, skids, sirens, gunshots, crashes and a moody pad loop — with no audio files. Title, pause, wasted and busted screens; progress autosaves and resumes.

Controls:

- **W A S D / arrow keys** — walk, or accelerate / brake / steer in a car
- **Shift** — sprint on foot · **Mouse** — aim · **Left click / J** — punch or shoot
- **R** — reload · **E** — enter / exit a car · **F** — talk, buy, start missions and races
- **Space** — handbrake · **P / Esc** — pause · **M** — mute · **Enter** — confirm

Everything — city, art, names, code — is original and procedural; no third-party assets are used.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts          # then open http://localhost:8000 in a desktop browser
```
